# Dana Thrive business card: Deep Green Natural

Finished size 90 × 55 mm, landscape, double-sided, 3 mm bleed on every side, 5 mm safe margin.

## Send to the printer
- `print/DanaThrive_BusinessCard_90x55_3mmBleed_CMYK.pdf`: **main print file.** Page 1 is the front, page 2 the back. 96 × 61 mm with bleed, CMYK, fonts embedded, TrimBox and BleedBox set.
- `print/DanaThrive_BusinessCard_90x55_CropMarks_CMYK.pdf`: the same artwork with crop marks, for printers that ask for marks.
- `print/DanaThrive_BusinessCard_90x55_3mmBleed_RGB.pdf`: RGB version, for online printers that ask for RGB.
- `print/DanaThrive_Front/Back_300dpi_withBleed.png`: 300 dpi images (1134 × 722 px) for upload-only services.
- `print/DanaThrive_Mockup_Preview.png`: preview only. Do not print.

## Checks
- The QR code links to https://danathrive.com.au/ (version 4, error correction H, 18 mm, 0.55 mm modules, 4-module quiet zone). It was decoded successfully from the CMYK PDF and from images down to 90 dpi.
- Maximum total ink coverage is 278%, within the usual 300% limit.
- Smallest text is 6 pt. Body text is cream on deep green, about 12:1 contrast.

## Before printing, confirm
- Social handles `@DanaThrive` (Instagram and TikTok) and `Dana Thrive` (Facebook) come from the reference design. The website project has no social links, so check these are your real accounts.
- Square corners are standard. For the rounded corners in the reference image, ask the printer for 3 mm round-corner die cutting. The artwork already allows for it.
- The gold is printed ink, not foil. For real gold foil, ask the printer for a foil mask; the gold elements can be separated onto their own layer.
